# Project charter

## Vision
Develop Mediora toward a reliable multimodal health assistant.

## Proposed first use case
Support clinicians assessing stable adults with suspected asthma.

## Intended user
To confirm with clinical partner.

## Clinical setting
To confirm.

## Inputs
- Structured symptom and medical history
- Available lung-function measurements
- Blood results, particularly eosinophils

## Proposed outputs
- Organized evidence summary
- Missing-information and data-quality flags
- Research assessment for clinician review

## Initial exclusions
- Autonomous diagnosis
- Prescribing
- Acute emergency triage
- Pediatric assessment
- Future attack forecasting
- Custom scanning or blood-testing hardware

## Primary research question
Does adding blood eosinophil information to history and
lung-function data improve asthma assessment?

## Success criteria
Define with supervisor, clinician and statistical adviser.

## Open decisions
- Clinical partner
- Dataset and reference standard
- Recruitment population
- Primary evaluation metric
- Ethics and access arrangements
- Thesis timeline
- IP ownership and commercialization rights
