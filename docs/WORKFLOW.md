# Working agreements

- Notion: tasks, milestones and risks.
- Obsidian: personal research synthesis and journal.
- Google Docs: collaborative proposal, protocol and manuscript.
- Zotero: bibliography and paper PDFs.
- GitHub: code, lockfile and approved research snapshots.
- Google Drive: approved shared documents and exports.

Do not maintain competing editable copies of the same document.
Keep patient data out of ordinary notes, Git, AI chats and
consumer cloud folders unless the institution approves that use.
